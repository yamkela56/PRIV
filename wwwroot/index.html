PRIV

PRIV is a privacy-first scheduling platform that lets you book time with anyone without exposing your calendar. Unlike traditional schedulers that show when and why you're busy, PRIV only displays computed "Available" slots like 2PM, 3PM, and 5PM — no meeting names, no private details. Users are discovered by unique username or shareable link (/u/username), request access, and once approved, can send booking requests with a type and 1-3 location options. The receiver approves by selecting one location as the confirmed spot, and if declined or cancelled, a reason is required and the slot is automatically freed.
